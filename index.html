The problem

Your business is held together by tabs, spreadsheets, and memory.

Most teams don't have a software problem. They have a "nothing talks to anything" problem — and people are the glue.

✕

Staff copy data between tools by hand. The same customer detail gets typed into three systems, and one of them is always out of date.

✕

Requests arrive in too many places. Email, phone, DMs, forms, walk-ins — and someone has to notice each one.

✕

Follow-up depends on someone remembering. When they're busy or away, things quietly fall through.

✕

Reporting takes hours. The numbers exist, but they're scattered across exports, inboxes, and someone's personal spreadsheet.

✕

Off-the-shelf software never matches the real process. So the team works around it — back into spreadsheets, back into memory.

The outcome

Software that fits the way your team actually works.

We don't sell a platform. We build the specific system your process needs, then connect it to the tools you already use.

Custom internal apps

Purpose-built screens for your workflow — intake, scheduling, tracking — instead of forcing your process into someone else's product.

Automated handoffs

When something happens in one tool, the next step starts in another — no copying, no "did you see my email?"

AI agents on the busywork

Agents that draft, summarize, classify, and route work — always producing a first pass for a human to approve, not acting unsupervised.

Dashboards that show what matters

One view of what's open, what's stuck, and what needs a person today — instead of a Monday-morning spreadsheet ritual.

FAQ

Sensible questions, straight answers.

Do we need technical knowledge?+
No. You need to know your own process — that's it. We handle the technical side, and everything we hand over is built so a non-technical team can run it day to day.
Can this connect to our existing tools?+
Usually, yes. Most modern tools — email, calendars, forms, spreadsheets, CRMs, accounting software — offer integrations we can build against. During the workflow audit we confirm exactly what your tools support before we commit to a design.
Do you build custom apps or only automations?+
Both. Some problems are solved by connecting the tools you already have; others need a small custom app with its own screens and database. We recommend whichever is simpler to run — not whichever is bigger to build.
Can AI agents review and draft work before humans approve it?+
Yes — and that's our default design. Agents draft replies, summaries, and classifications as a first pass, and a person approves before anything is sent or finalized. Nothing customer-facing goes out without human sign-off unless you explicitly choose otherwise.
How long does a typical build take?+
It depends on scope, which is why we start with the audit. A focused automation is typically a matter of weeks; a custom app with several connected workflows takes longer. After the audit you get a concrete timeline for your specific build — we don't quote before we've seen the process.
What should we automate first?+
The task that is frequent, repetitive, and rule-based — the one someone does every day while thinking "a machine should do this." That's usually intake, follow-up, or reporting. Bring it to the discovery call and we'll map it together.
